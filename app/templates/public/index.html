What it does

  • Log every expense

    Amount, category, date and an optional note. A running total shows the month so far as you type.

  • See where it goes

    Monthly totals and a breakdown by category, from groceries to travel.

  • Plan next month

    A forecast for each category, based on your last three full months.

  • Spot the odd one out

    Expenses far outside your usual pattern for a category are flagged, so mistakes and surprises stand out.
